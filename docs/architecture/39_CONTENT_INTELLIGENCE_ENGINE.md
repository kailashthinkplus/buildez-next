# Content Intelligence Engine

## Purpose

Content Intelligence defines strategy before copywriting. It does not mean "LLM writes text."

## Responsibilities

Define message hierarchy, headline strategy, section messaging roles, CTA strategy, proof strategy, FAQ strategy, SEO content strategy, trust copy, objection handling, locality content, compliance-sensitive copy rules, missing content facts, and content truth policy.

## Inputs

Business intelligence, brand intelligence, ontology, repository content patterns, SEO rules, compliance needs, known facts, and missing facts.

## Output

`ContentStrategy`.

## Multi-Industry Examples

- Real estate: location, project promise, configurations, amenities, site visit CTA, and no fake availability/RERA/prices.
- Healthcare: trust and credentials before appointment CTA; no unsupported outcomes.
- Restaurant: menu, ambience, location, hours, and reservation/order path early.
- Automotive: services/inventory, proof, booking/test-drive CTA, and no false authorization claims.
- Education: programs, outcomes proof, admissions path, and no fake placement claims.

## Failure Modes

- Writing copy before deciding proof and truth policy.
- Treating SEO as keyword stuffing.
- Inventing FAQs, prices, credentials, awards, or statistics.

## Implementation Guidance

The strategy should produce content requirements, not final copy. Copy generation, if added later, must be constrained by this strategy and facts used.
