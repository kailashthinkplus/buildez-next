# Inspiration Engine

## Purpose

The Inspiration Engine defines reference metadata for art direction. It helps BuildEZ describe creative direction without copying websites, assets, layouts, copy, or protected expression.

## Principle

Inspiration is metadata, not imitation.

The engine may describe qualities such as editorial pacing, cinematic real-estate mood, clinical clarity, sensory restaurant imagery, or industrial precision. It must not reproduce another site, brand, proprietary campaign, or exact composition.

## Inputs

- Business and brand intelligence.
- Content and experience strategy.
- Pattern intelligence.
- Design Engine result.
- Repository creative references.
- Tenant-provided brand assets.
- Missing asset facts.

## Output

`InspirationProfile`:

- inspiration themes
- reference categories
- do-not-copy constraints
- suitable creative territories
- unsuitable territories
- industry-safe examples
- confidence and missing inspiration facts

## Examples

- Real estate: "editorial residential project brochure", "calm luxury location storytelling", not a copied developer site.
- Healthcare: "clinical reassurance and high legibility", not a copied hospital page.
- Restaurant: "warm hospitality and sensory dining cues", not copied food photography.
- Automotive: "precision, engineering, performance energy", not copied manufacturer branding.
- Education: "trustworthy aspirational admissions narrative", not fake placement proof.
- Hospitality: "destination-first cinematic inspiration", not copied resort campaign.
- Interior design: "portfolio-led materials and spatial calm", not copied studio case study.
- D2C: "product-led editorial commerce", not copied product launch page.

## Failure Modes

- Treating inspiration as template selection.
- Copying visual structure from a specific website.
- Letting a provider decide the creative direction.
- Using inspiration to smuggle fake claims or unavailable assets.
