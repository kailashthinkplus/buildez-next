# ADR: Brand Intelligence Separate From Design

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Design Engine owns visual expression, but brand identity is broader than tokens.

## Problem

If brand and design are conflated, colors and typography start carrying business identity decisions they cannot justify.

## Decision

Brand Intelligence models personality, voice, tone, trust posture, differentiation, energy, premium level, risks, constraints, and assets before Design Engine creates visual output.

## Alternatives Considered

- Let Design Engine infer brand. Rejected.
- Store brand as prompt copy. Rejected.

## Consequences

Brand profile feeds WebsiteDNA, Content, Experience, Pattern Intelligence, and Design.

## Future Implications

Brand asset analysis can later strengthen this profile.
