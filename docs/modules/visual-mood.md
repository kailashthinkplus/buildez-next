# Module: Visual Mood Engine

## Purpose

Define visual mood attributes for future media and design execution.

## Output

`VisualMoodProfile`.

## Responsibilities

Define emotion, lighting, depth, texture, camera, material, contrast, image style, accessibility constraints, and missing mood facts.

## Examples

Healthcare mood is clear and low-risk; restaurant mood is warm and sensory; real estate mood is calm and premium; automotive mood is precise and high contrast; education mood is trustworthy and aspirational; hospitality mood is immersive; interior design mood is material and spatial; D2C mood is product-first.
