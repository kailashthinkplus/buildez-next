# Module: Inspiration Engine

## Purpose

Define inspiration metadata without copying websites.

## Output

`InspirationProfile`.

## Responsibilities

- Capture inspiration themes, reference categories, suitable/unsuitable creative territories, and do-not-copy constraints.
- Translate brand and industry context into safe art-direction references.
- Surface missing inspiration facts.

## Examples

- Real estate: editorial residential brochure, cinematic gallery rhythm.
- Healthcare: clinical clarity and reassurance.
- Restaurant: warm hospitality and tactile food cues.
- Automotive: precision engineering and performance.
- Education: aspirational admissions story.
- Hospitality: destination-led cinematic references.
- Interior design: material-led editorial portfolio.
- D2C: product-led campaign direction.
