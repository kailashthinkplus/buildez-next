# RC-3.5 Generalization Contract

## Universal widget contracts

Universal production code defines what a registered widget can safely express. Contracts cover roles, business-family compatibility, required props, item limits, verified facts, nested collections, editable Inspector paths, hydration rules, media slots, renderer shape, persistence expectations, and safe fallback policy.

These rules are independent of any business name or benchmark. The same population, hydration, media, persistence, and gate functions operate across every industry.

## Industry-role policies

Industry policies are declarative preferences, not fixed templates. They may prefer, allow, discourage, or forbid capabilities after considering business family, narrative/conversion role, media availability, and verified facts. They do not encode one universal widget sequence.

Eight certification contexts produce eight different sequences. Fact-dependent widgets remain ineligible without verified inputs regardless of industry.

## Generation-specific content

Business identity, audience, purpose, brand voice, art direction, page narrative, neighbouring sections, verified facts, known assets, missing facts, and the deterministic seed belong to generation context. Population compilers create scoped semantic slots; creative hydration supplies generation-specific language under typed schemas. Image providers fill declared media paths. None of this content is embedded in universal contracts.

Duplicate-copy validation uses normalized field fingerprints and section/widget identity. It evaluates generated values, not industry-specific hard-coded phrases.

## Fixture-only expectations

The Sanjeevini business, seed `104729`, forensic page/site identity, benchmark prompt/headlines, deterministic token replacement, and deliberately missing image output belong only to tests, forensic scripts/artifacts, and recovery documentation.

The fixture is expected to fail RC-3.5D because it leaves required hero media unresolved and repeats major copy. Production requirements were not weakened and no fixture-specific replacement was introduced. A production provider may populate those media slots, but provider failure still prevents a successful return.

Cross-industry certification uses deterministic synthetic contract inputs to prove gate behavior and sequence variation. It does not certify creative prose or rendered premium quality.
